Developing Software Solutions

Lectures

Keys: arrows or space - next and previous slide, Esc - overview of all the slides, F - full screen, S - speaker notes.

The slides with a green title are exercises for the class, on what the lecture has shown so far; the button Solution shows a solution. Every lecture ends with a larger assignment, solved in the folder exercises. A block of code has a button Copy (move the mouse over the block).

Under every group of lectures are the samples that its lectures use, as ZIP archives: download the archive when its module starts, unzip it, and open the folder in Visual Studio Code.
