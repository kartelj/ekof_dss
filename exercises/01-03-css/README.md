# Assignment: a page about a film - a solution

The assignment of the lecture [Introduction to CSS](../../presentations/01-03-css.md): a page about a film
(`film.html`), whose whole look is in its own style sheet (`film.css`).

- **The content**: a menu of three links, the title and a paragraph, a table of three actors and their roles,
  a list of three facts (one of them with the class `highlight`), and a footer.
- **The look**: the font and the colour set once for `<body>`, the links of the menu as boxes, a table with
  single lines and a row that lights up under the mouse, square markers, the main part in a box,
  relative sizes of the text, a small grey footer.

`film.css` is divided into six parts, in the order of the requirements on the slide *Assignment: the look*.

## How to open it

Copy the two files into your folder of the samples, `teaching-samples/01-html-css-bootstrap/wwwroot/my`,
start the server of the samples (`python main.py` in `teaching-samples/01-html-css-bootstrap`) and open
`http://localhost:8000/my/film.html`.

Or look at it on the web site of the course:
[kartelj.github.io/ekof_dss/exercises/01-03-css/film.html](https://kartelj.github.io/ekof_dss/exercises/01-03-css/film.html)

This is one solution of many: another film, other colours and other names of classes are just as good,
as long as the requirements are met.
